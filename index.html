Say this part plainly, because they already know

2026 is tracking to 14,259 games layoffs, worse than the January forecast of 8,025 and close to 2024’s record. Microsoft cut 3,200 gaming staff on July 6, the largest single event on record.

74% of student respondents to GDC’s State of the Game Industry said they are worried about job prospects. 87% of educators agree it is harder now. Pretending otherwise costs credibility.

And then say the part almost nobody is saying

There is a gap employers have openly admitted they are not going to close:

  • 50% of employers expect entry-level hires to arrive as practical AI users
  • 17% provide no AI training at all, and 55% offer only optional resources
  • Only 28% of the Class of 2026 say their school meaningfully integrated AI
  • 44% of interns got no AI onboarding whatsoever

That is the opening. A scholarship program that gives scholars structured, documented AI workflow practice is handing them the exact thing employers want and refuse to teach. You are literally building your own agent today. You are one cohort ahead of the market on this.

What a scholar does on Monday

1. Summer 2027 internship applications open September through December 2026 for a May start. Roblox is already open. That is inside our program year, so it goes on the September page and into Discord.

2. Keep a process log, not just a portfolio. Employers now want the decision evidenced. Shipped artifacts and documented process outrank credentials.

3. Write the paragraph: what AI did on this project, and what you rejected. That is a portfolio differentiator right now, and it is the December deliverable on the scholar page.

Exposure, honestly

Most exposed: junior coding (~20% employment decline for 22-25 year old devs, Stanford, ADP payroll data on 4.6M workers), customer support, and QA. QA is the historic entry door into studios, which is why the door feels shut.

Least exposed: anything where judgment and taste have to be defended out loud. Which is what mentor sessions and recruiter breakouts actually rehearse.

On sourcing. Every number above is measured, not forecast, except the 14,259 which is explicitly a year-end projection.
